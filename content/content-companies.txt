CAREERPASSPORT / FOR COMPANIES
Final Website Content & Experience Direction
A six-screen design handoff for the Companies page
CORE
CareerPassport takes a hiring manager's intent, turns it into a structured hiring journey, helps execute
that journey across candidates and partners, assembles the evidence, and brings humans in where
human judgment matters most.
FINAL HANDOFF | 6-SCREEN PAGE
NORTH STAR
One story. Six screens. One continuous product journey.
CareerPassport's story is strongest when the capabilities appear as one connected system, not as
isolated features.
01 — Hiring Intent
Understand what the organisation wants.
02 — Hiring Design
Create the role, competency framework, evaluation framework and Candidate Journey.
03 — Agentic Execution
Operate communications, progression, coordination and scheduling.
04 — Source-Agnostic Orchestration
Bring candidates from internal databases, partners or CareerPassport into one process.
05 — Evidence
Assemble richer signals and supporting proof.
06 — Human Judgment
Give the hiring manager better context for the decisions that need them.

Experience Principles
One object keeps evolving
The Hiring Blueprint becomes the role, the journey, the live workflow, the evidence view and finally the
interview brief.
The product does the explaining
Prefer visible product-state changes over long explanatory copy or separate feature grids.
The hiring manager stays the protagonist
Every screen should answer: What work disappears? What becomes clearer? Where does the manager
now add value?
Human judgment is the destination
Automation should culminate in a better human decision, not in the impression that AI is making the
hiring decision.

IMPLEMENTATION RULES
Keep the primary narrative to six screens. Do not add separate feature, integration, automation-
control or recruitment-partner sections unless evidence from user testing requires them.
Use scroll progression to transform the same product state. Avoid hard visual resets between
sections.
Keep candidate sources and partner orchestration visible inside Screen 03 so the source-
agnostic promise is understood without expanding the page.
Make autonomy visible through a mix of automatic agent events and selected human approvals
rather than a long explanation of controls.
Do not label candidates as “verified.” Show evidence, context and verification signals where
available; the company makes the judgment.
Do not let integrations become the proposition. Gmail, WhatsApp, LinkedIn and Calendar appear
as supporting execution channels.
The CTA hierarchy stays consistent from start to finish: Book a demo as primary; Post a role as
secondary.
DESIGNER CHECK
At every scroll point, the visitor should be able to answer:
What did CareerPassport just do for the hiring manager?
