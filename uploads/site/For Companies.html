Define

One intent becomes a hiring blueprint.

Describe the role in your own words.

CareerPassport assembles the structure the process needs.

One standard, applied to everyone who applies.

Design

Decide what you need to know before you meet them.

Candidates can be asked to demonstrate relevant experience, complete role-specific activities, provide evidence or undertake assessments before progressing.

Use CareerPassport-native journeys and integrate third-party assessments where required.

Activate

Once the role is live, agents keep hiring moving.

  • Engage candidates
  • Send follow-ups
  • Progress candidates
  • Coordinate assessments
  • Manage communication
  • Schedule next steps

Every candidate source runs through one process — your database, your internal recruiters, your recruitment partners and CareerPassport.

Evidence

See why someone is worth meeting.

A CV is one page of bullet points. A recruiter's note is one person's read.

There are projects behind those bullets.

And evidence behind the decisions, if anyone asks for it.

CareerPassport assembles the evidence.

Hiring manager make the judgment.

Decide
