CareerPassport for Recruitment Partners
Condensed 6-Screen Website Direction
Overall feeling: Premium, intelligent, controlled, commercially powerful.
Core visual idea: One private recruitment network evolves throughout the page. We never abandon it
and introduce random feature graphics. It begins as the recruiter's private database, becomes
intelligent, begins processing a role, gains agents, connects selectively to the marketplace, and
ultimately delivers a candidate to the client.

Do not represent agents as robots, people, avatars or AI assistants.
Agents should be represented as actions occurring within the workflow.

17 candidates identified
8 personalised messages sent
WhatsApp follow-up completed

2 candidates ready for review
Then show:
