========== PAGE 2 ==========
Design cue
The Passport cover is the hero object. As someone enters a handle, their handle name subtly appears
on the Passport. The interaction can then naturally move to email capture. The website shows the
availability and count of passport handle issued.
The first screen should make someone imagine owning their CareerPassport before explaining how it
works.
Transition : 
From right side passport on screen 1 to passport becoming the central object
And this statement comes "What you truly are has never been easier to paint."
Design thought
The hero should make the Passport itself feel like a desirable object.
Do not explain every feature.
Show enough to create intrigue: the person's identity, an experience, perhaps a Visa, an earned Stamp
and a glimpse of evidence.
The handle interaction should make the concept instantly personal:
careerpassport.ai/me
This screen should create desire before explanation.

========== PAGE 5 ==========
CareerPassport helps turn those moments into artifacts and evidence of what you can actually do.
And when that evidence demonstrates real depth, you can earn a Stamp that carries that signal
forward.
The loop
Connect what already exists
→ Talk about what you're living
→ Follow a curated journey
→ Create evidence
→ Earn meaningful signals
→ Grow your CareerPassport
Design cue
This should be one seamless visual loop rather than six feature cards.
Begin with existing resources flowing into the Passport.
Move naturally into a Companion conversation.
Let one interesting experience emerge from that conversation.
Show a small journey being suggested.
Show the resulting artifact or evidence.
Then let that evidence resolve into a Stamp and become part of the Passport.
The experience should feel effortless on the user's side:
talk, participate, keep moving.
The intelligence and structure happen around them.
Transition
So when something worth pursuing appears, you're already ready.
